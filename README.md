Directors Notes Context 
Directors Notes is an independent UK film publication that publishes short films alongside interviews exploring the decisions, craft and people behind them. The project aims to make the Directors Notes film archive more useful and discoverable. 
Chosen Brief 
My chosen brief focuses on displaying a small collection of films and allowing visitors to filter the collection using a shared characteristic. 
Intended Visitor 
The intended visitor is someone exploring the Directors Notes film archive who wants to discover and browse films based on a particular characteristic. 
Opportunity 
The project explores how a simple filtering feature could make it easier for visitors to discover films within a collection. 
Starting Point 
My goal is to display a small set of fictional film cards that all share one characteristic and allow the visitor to filter the collection using that characteristic. 
Asset Approach 
I will use fictional films and content created for this prototype. I will not use Directors Notes photographs, film stills, videos, logos, article text or archive data. 
Current Status 
The project is currently in the planning stage. I have defined the Starting Point and created an initial backlog of tasks. The next step is to plan the fictional film collection and begin developing the film cards.
